Behind the Camera

I know the mountain.

My name is Tim Hoheneder. I am a New Hampshire-based sports photographer and former collegiate snowsports athlete. That background helps me anticipate the action, understand the rhythm of an event, and work comfortably in the weather and terrain where winter sports happen.

I have worked alongside national teams, Olympic athletes, and some of the best competitors in the world. My goal is not simply to document who competed. It is to create photographs that make the speed, personality, and atmosphere of the event feel memorable.

That same approach carries into portrait work: understand the person, find the right setting, and create room for something real to happen in front of the camera.
